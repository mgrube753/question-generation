## Changes made

### Learning Objectives

In Experiment 2, two learning objectives were changed. Bloom1 and Bloom4 were affected here.

- Bloom1: "und zu jeder Schicht ihre Hauptfunktion beschreiben" instead of "jeder Schicht ihre Hauptfunktion zuordnen"
- Bloom4: "Gemeinsamkeiten und Unterschiede zwischen verwandten Konzepten verschiedener Layer gegenüberstellen" instead of "Unterschiede zwischen verwandten Konzepten verschiedener Layer differenzieren"

### Bloom Level Descriptions

- The initial descriptions were based on these of the Krathwohl (2002) paper [A Revision of Bloom's Taxonomy: An Overview](https://cmapspublic2.ihmc.us/rid=1Q2PTM7HL-26LTFBX-9YN8/Krathwohl%202002.pdf).
- These were less sufficient, so the descriptions were changed to the Bloom (1956) descriptions of [Taxonomy of Educational Objectives](https://eclass.uoa.gr/modules/document/file.php/PPP242/Benjamin%20S.%20Bloom%20-%20Taxonomy%20of%20Educational%20Objectives%2C%20Handbook%201_%20Cognitive%20Domain-Addison%20Wesley%20Publishing%20Company%20(1956).pdf). These are illustrated in [after_combined.docx](after_combined.docx).
- However, to enhance the wording and make it more consistent, we further changed the descriptions based on [Lernzieltaxonomische Klassifizierung und gezielte Gestaltung von Fragen](https://www.zfhe.at/index.php/zfhe/article/view/891/745) and (probably) the table [Taxonomiestufen nach BLOOM](https://paeda-logics.ch/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Taxonomiestufen_Bloom.pdf).
