Here, we tried to reduce the length of open-ended answers and make MCQ questions more meaningful/harmonious within the steps. However, this was archived because open-ended answers are still too long, and MCQ is still not completely harmonious. Our next step was then to adjust the prompts in such a way that we change the models' role (students of computer science in an exam situation, with a maximum word count / time limit for answering). For MCQ, it remained problematic to formulate the two keys semantically different. They often had the same in the respective answer.
